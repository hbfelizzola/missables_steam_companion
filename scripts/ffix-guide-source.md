YOU FAILED: Excalibur II

Before we even step into the shoes of Zidane it is important that you have your expectations set in regards to completion.   
  
You see, Square decided to add a little bit of a speedrun challenge to this game, where you can only get this Ultimate Weapon by making it to its location in the final dungeon within 12 hours.  
  
Technically speaking this is not missable, in fact you may even have time to spare if you are somewhat familiar with the game. However, it would be safe to assume you will miss it if it is your first playthrough.  
  
Lastly, if your OCD is already triggering, you can circumvent the time limit by overflowing the in-game timer so that it goes back to 00:00:00 after keeping the game open for 2.92 years. Or, you know... by committing the cardinal sin of cheating in a single player game from literally the year 2000\. It'll be our little secret ;)  
  
Unrevisitable Locations & Treasure Hunter Rank

On the topic of wasting time to ensure we do not get Excalibur II, this game wants you to collect 75% of the treasures it offers which includes treasure chests found in the areas, every key item in the Auction House and chests found while riding a Chocobo.  
  
To add insult to injury, this game has several areas and sections of areas that become inaccessible at various points in the game, for your own mental sanity please just assume all areas of the game are unrevisitable and be as diligent as possible on your first visit as this guide will not tell you where every chest is cause at that point I may as well write a Walkthrough instead.  
  
It's All in the Cards

Yet another reason to fail Excalibur II's time requirement, you need to defeat 100 different opponents in Tetra Master for three achievements, fight everyone you meet like we are playing Yu-Gi-Oh!  
  
Mognet Deliveries

Through the course of the game you will be able to help with the mognet letter delivery. Almost all of the letters are missable and so they are documented in this guide. The last few are actually required for a sidequest as well so you should do those at the very least. You have been warned.  
  
These will be labeled as "Mognet (Sender to Recipient START/END)" for each of them.  
  
ATE

Active Time Events are optional scenes you can watch to get a bit more of that juicy world building. Some of them have pre-requisites and can be easy to miss.  
  
Do note this guide will not include screenshots even when we talk about each of the 79\* bloody things, we can pretend I'm trying to save digital paper or something.  
\*You may notice this guide has 82 in total, this is because all three "Teach Me, Mogster! Lesson #" ATEs do not count for the achievement.  
  
Lastly, The letter "M" indicates an ATE is mandatory.  
  
Gil Cheat

You may not want to use this cheat until after watching ATE 075 since it requires for you to have less than 50 Gil and spending all of it will be complicated.  
  
Save Slots

Use them. That's it... this section's only purpose is the first -and last- friendly reminder that this game loves you and is giving you enough save slots for an entire household to play it multiple runs each, so the least you can do is use them.  
  
Also, do try to prioritize the missables over anything else, this will save you headaches having to reload any of the aforementioned backups.

Festival of the Hunt

We will partake in a small minigame and get a reward depending on the winner. Make sure whoever wins does so with over 100 points for a letter later in the game, not that I think its possible to win below that though.  
* Zidane: Gil 5,000 Gil
* Freya: Add-on Coral Ring
* Vivi: Card Theater Ship + Steam Achievement
It is worth noting that Zidane will not get the other reward he thinks he would, so you can choose purely based on convenience, here are the conditions:  
* Zidane: Beat as many monsters as you can, make sure to have Zidane deliver the killing blow to the Zaghnol which will spawn once you enter the fountain area on the Business District with the timer below 04:30.
* Freya: Have Zidane KO'd at any time prior to the Zaghnol fight.
* Vivi: Have both Zidane and Freya KO'd while fighting the Zaghnol.

Quina's Blu Mag

Quina learns blu mag by eating things, this can be done through commands which will succeed when the monster's HP is below a specific percentage.  
* Eat: At 25% or less.
* Cook: At 50% or less.

**EXP Farm: Grand Dragon**  
We need to do a little bit of setup before we begin, make sure to save once its ready:  
1. Have Quina be at 1 HP.  
You can get this done by simply letting her die and reviving her with a Phoenix Down or Auto-Life.
2. Find a Grand Dragon.
3. Cast Night to make it fall asleep.
4. Spam Limit Glove for 9,999 damage every turn until the dragon is no longer alive.
  
Alternatively, let your fingers slip into enabling the max damage cheat and forget all of the above.

 Minigames 

Frog Catching

You can play this minigame by going to any of the four Marshes across the world.  
  
**How to play**  
Just catch frogs, that's it, that's the game you are voluntarily playing.  
  
**Tips**  
Couple of things to keep in mind to avoid headaches.  
* Play this minigame whenever you have the chance while going through the story.
* Do not spam it though as the frogs have a whole mechanic dedicated to their growth.
* Do not catch all frogs either. Leave at least 1 male, 1 female and 1 golden (if present).  
Female frogs have a black stripe through their bodies, red feet and are slightly lighter shade.
* Save before catching frog 99.
* Reload the game after catching a gold frog for the first time, if you care about achievements.
  
**Rewards**  
Your goodies depend on the number of frogs captured:  
* 02 - Ore
* 05 - Ether
* 09 - Silk Robe
* 15 - Elixir
* 23 - Silver Fork
* 33 - Bistro Fork
* 45 - Battle Boots
* 99 - Boss Fight against Quale for Quina's Ultimate Weapon: Gastro Fork.
Plus you get to power up Quina's Blu Mag's Frog Drop for each one captured, which uses the formula below. Go nuts.  
Quina's Level \* Number of frogs caught.
