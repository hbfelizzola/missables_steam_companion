 FFX: Introduction 

Self esteem booster

Remember to give it a thumbs up and favorite if it helped you, I need that stuff for self validation :)  
  
Save Slots

Use them. That's it... this section's only purpose is the first -and last- friendly reminder that this game loves you and is giving you enough save slots for an entire household to play it multiple teams each, so the least you can do is use them.  
  
Also, do try to prioritize the missables over anything else, this will save you headaches having to reload any of the aforementioned backups.  
  
Missables

They are all marked with a ⚠ to at the beginning of the entry to keep things simple.  
  
Sphere Grid

I recommend the Standard Sphere Grid but the choice is ultimately yours. You should be able to beat everything with either one regardless of difficulty.  
  
**Standard**  
860 nodes, with 85 unremovable nodes and 775 manipulable nodes.  
Allows you to have overall superior stats during the endgame but everyone follows their intended job path for the main story.  
  
**Expert**  
805 nodes with 85 unremovable nodes and 720 manipulable nodes.  
Allows you to quickly customize your characters to your exact needs during the main story since everyone shares starting point and there are less obstructions in the Grid, but there are also less nodes to customize during the endgame.  
  
Cheats

This game has built-in cheats that can be accessed by pressing ESC on the keyboard.  
  
I only mention this since controller players may never find them and have no way of using them naturally unless installing the Untitled Project X mod.

Nemesis

Defeat all Area Conquest, Species Conquest and Original monsters to unlock the final fight of the Arena.  
  
  
Gear Prep

As we advance make our way through the Arena we want to start buying and customizing the Tetra armor pieces for all our party members. These are bought from Wantz near the entrance of \[Macalania Woods - South\] for 100,000 Gil each.  
  
Auto-Haste is no negotiable, other than that it's really your choice for the other three slots.  
  
**Note**: You could get the weapons too if you feel under-prepared, but the Arena will open up the grind for max stats so it's not that important, especially since the Celestial Weapons will make them obsolete "soon" enough.   
Not to mention the initial portion of the Arena requires to use weapons with Capture so...  
  
Max Stats

Now that we completed the tutorial grind we can get started on the real grind!  
* Standard Grid: You can max basically all stats, minus two if you go for Break HP Limit.
* Expert Grid: You need to min-max, Luck is the best stat in the game.
* 255 is the cap for each stat except HP and MP.
* Stats: 255 / 4 = 63.75
* HP caps at 9,999 and MP at 999\. These values go up to 99,999 and 9,999 if you break the limit.
* HP: 99,999 / 300 = 333.33 | 9,999 / 300 = 33.33
* MP: 9,999 / 40 = 249.975 | 999 / 40 = 24.975
* To keep things simple get everyone Mug and never attack normally again.
**Change Nodes**  
* HP Sphere: Ironclad.
* MP Sphere: Vidatu.
* Strength Sphere: Juggernaut.
* Defense Sphere: Tanket.
* Magic Sphere: Jumbo Flan.
* Magic Defense Sphere: One Eye.
* Agility Sphere: Fenrir.
* Luck Sphere: Greater Sphere.
* Evasion Sphere: Pteryx.
* Accuracy Sphere: Hornet.
* Clear Sphere: Buy.
**Activate Nodes**  
* Kottos: Use an Extract Skill or Distiller to get 20\~40 of the respective stat.
* Fortune Sphere: Earth Eater.

Celestial Mirror

To get started we need to turn the Cloudy Mirror that we got at Remiem Temple into the Celestial Mirror following a tiny questline. Otherwise we won't be able to open any of the weapon chests.   
1. Find the Mother and Son at the entrance of Macalania Woods - South to the right of the Save Sphere.
2. Take a right twice and then head up to find the Husband at Macalania Woods - Campsite. Then go back to the family and exhaust their dialogue like this was a Dark Souls NPC sidequest.
3. Take the magical shiny path right next to you to find the Son at Macalania Woods - Central and use the Cloudy Mirror to turn it into the Celestial Mirror.

 Overdrives 

Mode

By far the most annoying grind in the entire game in my opinion, also the most useless one.  
So, Overdrive Modes are unlocked after fulfilling their conditions a number of times per each party member while fighting outside of the Monster Arena cause ♥♥♥♥ you.  
  
For example: Yuna learns the Healer Mode faster than anyone else by healing allies, while Auron learns Coward Mode slower than anyone else by fleeing from combat.  
  
I will not bother listing the number of times, but do know that most modes expect around a hundred, with the longest grind being Auron's Coward which needs a nice 1,000 repetitions.  
* Stoic: Character takes damage.
* Warrior: Character damages an enemy.
* Comrade: Allies take damage.
* Healer: Character heals allies.
* Tactician: Status attack succeeds.
* Victim: Character hit with status attack.
* Dancer: Character evades attack.
* Avenger: Allies are KO'd.
* Slayer: Character defeats an enemy.
* Hero: Character defeats powerful enemy.
* Rook: Character magically blocks an attack.
* Victor: Party wins a battle.
* Coward: Character flees from battle.
* Ally: Character's turn.
* Sufferer: Character suffers status ailments.
* Daredevil: Character fights with low HP.
* Loner: Character fights alone.
